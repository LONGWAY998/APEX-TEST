Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Apex Capital ("we," "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data.

1. Data Collection

We may collect the following categories of personal data:
  • Identity Data: name, ID number, passport number, date of birth
  • Contact Data: address, telephone number, email address
  • Financial Data: bank account information, proof of income, asset position, investment experience
  • Transaction Data: subscription and redemption records, holding information
  • Due Diligence Data: information required for anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) procedures

2. Data Usage

Your personal data is used solely for the following purposes:
  • Processing fund subscriptions, redemptions, and distributions
  • Fulfilling legal and regulatory obligations (including AML screening)
  • Providing client service and product updates
  • Risk management and internal audit

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data may be shared only in the following circumstances:
  • With regulators, exchanges, and law enforcement as required by law
  • With service partners (e.g., custodian banks, auditors) bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations
  • With your explicit consent in other situations

4. Data Security

We employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including data encryption, access controls, and periodic security audits. Data is stored on domestic servers; cross-border transfers require compliance approval.

5. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for a minimum of 7 years following the termination of the business relationship, in accordance with regulatory requirements. Data is securely destroyed upon expiry of the retention period.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and correct your personal data
  • Restrict or object to data processing
  • Withdraw consent (without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing)
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority
