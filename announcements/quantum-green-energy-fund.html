New Fund Launch

Quantum Green Energy Fund

June 15, 2026 Xiamen, China 5 min read

Jinnong Sui Capital is proud to announce the launch of the Quantum Green Energy Fund, a thematic investment vehicle focused on the global energy transition. The fund targets high-growth opportunities across solar, wind, energy storage, and hydrogen value chains, combining quantitative screening with fundamental analysis.

Investment Strategy

The fund employs a multi-factor quantitative model to screen over 800 listed companies in the clean energy sector worldwide. The portfolio is concentrated in 30–50 high-conviction positions, rebalanced quarterly. Risk management includes sector exposure caps at 25% and single-position limits at 8%.
