Keynote

Future of Digital Assets

April 22, 2026 Hong Kong Convention Centre 6 min read

At the 2026 Digital Finance Forum in Hong Kong, Jinnong Sui Capital's CEO delivered a widely anticipated keynote on the institutional adoption of digital assets and the transformative role of blockchain technology in traditional finance. The speech outlined a pragmatic roadmap for integrating digital assets into regulated fund structures.

"Digital assets are no longer a niche experiment — they are becoming a structural component of the global financial system. The question is not whether to engage, but how to do so with the same rigor and fiduciary discipline we apply to every other asset class."
— Jinnong Sui Capital CEO, 2026 Digital Finance Forum

Jinnong Sui Capital's Approach

The keynote outlined a three-phase approach: Phase 1 (2026) — research and education, building internal expertise; Phase 2 (2027) — pilot allocations in select managed accounts with strict exposure limits; Phase 3 (2028+) — dedicated digital asset fund products subject to regulatory approval. All initiatives will operate within existing compliance frameworks, including AML/KYC, custody segregation, and independent audit.
