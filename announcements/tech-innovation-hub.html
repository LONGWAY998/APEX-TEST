Strategic Partnership

Tech Innovation Hub Alliance

May 8, 2026 Shenzhen, China 4 min read

Apex Capital has entered into a strategic partnership with the Tech Innovation Hub, a leading fintech accelerator based in Shenzhen. This collaboration aims to integrate cutting-edge artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities into our investment research and risk management platforms.

Tech Innovation Hub

Partnership Scope

Key Initiatives

  • AI-powered credit risk modeling for bond and bill portfolios
  • Natural language processing for regulatory filing analysis
  • Real-time market sentiment dashboard for trading desks
  • Blockchain-based settlement prototype for interbank CD transactions

The partnership grants Apex Capital access to a talent pool of over 200 AI researchers and engineers, along with state-of-the-art GPU computing infrastructure. In return, Apex Capital will provide real-world financial datasets and domain expertise to help validate and commercialize the Hub's research.

Expected Outcomes

Within the first 12 months, we expect to deploy an AI-assisted credit screening module that reduces manual review time by 60% while improving default prediction accuracy. The NLP system will monitor over 5,000 regulatory filings per day across Chinese and English sources for compliance and investment signals.
